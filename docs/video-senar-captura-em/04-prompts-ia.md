# Prompts para geração fotorrealista — Categoria Especial

> **Leia antes de gerar.** O edital separa as categorias com rigor (itens 2.2.7 e 2.2.8):
> **Especial** = conteúdo visual e audiovisual **integralmente** gerado por IA.
> Se você misturar uma única cena filmada de verdade, o vídeo tem de ser inscrito na
> **Tradicional** — e a divergência entre a categoria informada e o conteúdo submetido
> **desclassifica**. Decida a categoria antes de começar a produzir, não depois.
>
> **Exceção que não quebra a regra:** cartelas, legendas e o logo institucional são
> pós-produção/identidade visual, não "cena". Elas entram nas duas categorias.

---

## Ficha de consistência (cole em toda geração)

Antes de gerar qualquer plano, fixe estes três elementos e **não os mude no meio do vídeo**
— inconsistência entre cenas é penalizada no critério VII (qualidade visual).

**PERSONAGEM (defina uma vez e repita literalmente):**

```
Brazilian rural extension instructor, mid-40s, sun-weathered face, short dark hair,
wearing a long-sleeved light-blue cotton work shirt, dark work trousers, a wide-brim
straw hat, brown leather high-top work boots and green cloth-and-nitrile gardening
gloves. Calm, competent, unhurried body language.
```

**AMBIENTE:**

```
Brazilian Cerrado transition woodland in Goiás, small family farm, warm humid morning,
soft natural sunlight filtering through the canopy, red-brown lateritic soil,
thick layer of dry leaf litter.
```

**ESTILO (fixo em todos os planos):**

```
photorealistic documentary cinematography, shot on full-frame camera, 35mm lens,
natural light only, shallow depth of field, subtle handheld motion, 16:9 horizontal,
1920x1080, 30fps, film grain minimal, no on-screen text, no logos, no watermarks,
no subtitles burned in.
```

**NEGATIVOS (em toda geração):**

```
no text, no captions, no logos, no brand names, no distorted hands, no extra fingers,
no floating objects, no cartoon, no illustration, no oversaturated colors,
no people without gloves in the woodland scenes, no recognizable real person.
```

## Plano a plano

Cada bloco abaixo é um prompt independente. Gere **2 a 3 variações de cada** e escolha a
melhor — sai mais barato do que tentar consertar uma geração ruim na edição.

---

### 01 · Abertura — 7s

```
Extreme close-up, low camera angle at ground level: a gloved human hand slowly sweeps
aside a thick layer of dry brown leaf litter on a forest floor, revealing dark, moist,
crumbly living soil underneath. Backlit morning sun rays cut through the woodland
canopy behind, creating soft golden haze. Tiny particles of dust float in the light.
Macro focus on the soil texture, background falls into creamy bokeh.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

### 02 · O que é o EM — 10s (duas gerações, corte seco entre elas)

**02a (4s):**
```
Macro shot: a cupped gloved hand holds a handful of dark forest soil mixed with
fragments of decomposing leaves and fine white fungal threads. The hand turns slowly.
Shallow woodland background, soft dappled light.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

**02b (6s):**
```
Wide-to-medium shot: [PERSONAGEM] crouches at the edge of a cultivated vegetable
plot on a small Brazilian farm, letting dark soil fall between the fingers of a gloved
hand. Rows of leafy greens and straw mulch behind. Bright open morning sunlight,
distant woodland treeline on the horizon.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

### 03 · Passo 1 — o arroz — 12s (três gerações)

**03a (4s):**
```
Kitchen counter in a simple Brazilian farmhouse. Close-up of an open aluminium pot of
freshly cooked plain white rice, steam rising gently in the air. Warm side light from
a window. No seasoning, no colour in the rice — pure white.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

**03b (3s):**
```
Close-up of a small digital kitchen scale on a wooden counter holding a bowl of cooked
white rice. Natural window light. Clean, simple, rustic setting.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

**03c (5s):**
```
Overhead top-down shot: two hands spread cooked white rice evenly into a thin flat
layer across a rectangular wooden tray, using a wooden spoon. The layer is deliberately
shallow and uniform. Rustic wooden table, warm natural light from the side.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

### 04 · Passo 2 — a tela — 8s

```
Close-up: two hands stretch a fine mesh screen (mosquito-net style) over a wooden tray
filled with a thin layer of cooked white rice, tucking the edges down along the sides.
Then a macro detail of the mesh weave backlit by soft sunlight, the rice visible
underneath, slightly out of focus.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

### 05 · Passo 3 — na mata — 16s (quatro gerações, é o bloco mais importante)

**05a (4s):**
```
Medium tracking shot from behind: [PERSONAGEM] walks along the edge of a Brazilian
woodland carrying a wooden tray covered with mesh screen. Full protective clothing
clearly visible — long sleeves, long trousers, straw hat, gloves and high-top leather
boots. Dappled morning light, leaf litter crunching underfoot.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

**05b (4s):**
```
Close-up, low angle: gloved hands sweep aside thick dry leaf litter on the woodland
floor, clearing a flat rectangular patch of bare dark soil, forming a shallow bed.
Soft dappled sunlight through the canopy.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

**05c (4s):**
```
Close-up: gloved hands set the mesh-covered wooden tray of rice down flat onto the
cleared patch of forest soil, pressing it lightly so it sits level and stable.
Woodland floor, dappled light.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

**05d (4s):**
```
Close-up then slow pull back to a wide shot: gloved hands gently return handfuls of dry
leaf litter over the mesh screen until the tray is completely hidden. Camera pulls back
to reveal the woodland edge — the spot has disappeared into the forest floor,
indistinguishable from its surroundings.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

### 06 · Passo 4 — a espera — 9s (duas gerações)

**06a (4s) — passagem de tempo:**
```
Static locked-off shot of the same woodland floor spot covered in leaf litter, with a
time-lapse feeling: light shifting from dawn to midday to dusk repeatedly, shadows
sweeping across the ground, a brief rain shower darkening the leaves, then drying.
No people. Same framing throughout.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

**06b (5s) — a revelação:**
```
Overhead top-down macro shot: gloved hands lift the mesh screen off a wooden tray,
revealing cooked rice now completely covered in dense fuzzy microbial colonies in
distinct patches of white, cream, pale orange, pink, grey and pale green. Rich texture,
soft natural daylight, high detail on the colony surfaces.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

> **Rigor técnico:** as cores devem parecer **colônias em placas irregulares**, não
> arroz tingido. Se a geração sair com aparência de comida colorida, refaça — o
> avaliador do Senar conhece o material.

### 07 · Passo 5 — a ativação — 14s (três gerações)

**07a (4s):**
```
Rustic workbench under a farm shed roof. Five clean empty 2-litre transparent plastic
bottles stand in a row. Warm afternoon light from the side. Simple, orderly, practical.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

**07b (5s):**
```
Close-up: gloved hands use a plastic funnel to transfer colourful mould-covered rice
from a wooden tray into a 2-litre transparent plastic bottle, then pour thick dark
molasses from a graduated measuring cup into the same bottle. The molasses falls in a
slow glossy ribbon. Shallow depth of field, warm natural light.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

**07c (5s):**
```
Close-up: water is poured from a jug into the plastic bottle, filling it. Beside it, an
open uncovered bucket of water sits on the bench in the background. Warm shed light,
water surface catching highlights.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

### 08 · Passo 6 — a fermentação — 13s (três gerações)

**08a (4s):**
```
Close-up: two gloved hands squeeze the sides of a filled 2-litre plastic bottle until
the liquid rises into the neck, expelling the air, then quickly screw the cap on tight.
The bottle stays visibly dented inward. Warm shed light.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

**08b (4s):**
```
Medium shot: five filled plastic bottles of dark liquid standing in the shade on a
wooden shelf under a farm shed roof, away from direct sun. Cool shaded light,
warm sunlit yard visible out of focus in the background.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

**08c (5s):**
```
Close-up profile shot: a gloved hand loosens the cap of a pressurised plastic bottle,
holding the bottle out to the side and clearly away from the person's face, head turned
away. Gas escapes with visible vapour and the dented bottle visibly expands back to its
shape. Shaded natural light. The safe technique must be unmistakable.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

### 09 · Como saber que deu certo — 9s

```
Close-up: a 2-litre plastic bottle held up against soft backlight, filled with a
translucent amber-orange liquid with fine sediment settled at the bottom, glowing
warmly. Then the hand uncaps it and the person leans in, wafting the vapour toward
the nose with the other hand — the correct way to smell a sample. Satisfied expression.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

### 10 · Como usar — 8s (duas gerações)

**10a (3s):**
```
Close-up: a graduated one-litre jug of amber liquid is poured into a large blue water
tank / knapsack sprayer reservoir on a farm. Practical, warm late-afternoon light.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

**10b (5s):**
```
Wide shot, golden hour: [PERSONAGEM] walks along a straw-mulched vegetable bed on a
small Brazilian farm, watering it evenly with a large watering can. Long warm shadows,
low golden sun, dust and mist in the air. Healthy green crops.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

### 11 · Chamada Senar — 10s

```
Wide-to-medium shot, golden hour on a small Brazilian farm: a group of eight adult
rural producers of mixed ages and genders stand in a semicircle in a field, attentive,
while [PERSONAGEM] holds up a bottle of amber liquid and explains, gesturing. Some
producers hold notebooks, one holds a bottle and examines it. Genuine engagement,
smiles, hands-on learning. Warm low sunlight, crops and woodland treeline behind.
[ESTILO] [NEGATIVOS]
```

> **Atenção ao item 5.4 do edital:** não pode haver criança sem autorização do
> responsável legal. Gere **apenas adultos** nesta cena e não haverá o problema.
> Nenhum figurante pode parecer uma pessoa real identificável.

## Voz sintética (se a locução for por IA)

O edital avalia, na Categoria Especial, "a naturalidade e entonação de vozes sintéticas"
(critério VIII). Três ajustes que resolvem a maior parte do problema:

1. **Voz brasileira, sotaque neutro**, timbre médio, idade aparente 35–50.
2. **Velocidade a 0,95×** do padrão. A voz sintética soa apressada no ritmo nominal, e a
   locução didática precisa de espaço.
3. **Gere bloco a bloco**, não o texto inteiro de uma vez. Blocos curtos entoam melhor e
   permitem refazer só o trecho ruim. Junte na edição com as pausas do roteiro.

Números por extenso no texto entregue ao sintetizador — "setecentos gramas", "duzentos
mililitros", "dez a quinze dias". O arquivo `02-narracao.txt` já está assim.
